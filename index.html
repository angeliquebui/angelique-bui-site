About me

Your customer, creating for your brand.

I live at the intersection of two worlds you don't often see together — fitness and finance. I train clients in the morning and analyze startup investments in the afternoon. That mix gives my content a different feel: polished, credible, and genuinely relatable — never overly scripted.

As an on-camera fitness coach, I'm used to showing up on camera in a way that feels clear, motivating, and genuinely human. Whether I’m guiding workouts, explaining concepts, or helping users stay engaged, I know how to communicate naturally on camera.

Outside of fitness, I also work in finance, giving me a unique perspective on ambitious, health-conscious consumers who care deeply about both wellness and personal growth.

Based in San Francisco, I create polished, creator-led content for fitness, wellness, fintech, and modern lifestyle brands looking for someone who can both represent and genuinely understand their audience.
